# LinkedIn post

I just finished my Google Data Analytics capstone, and I loved this one.

I analyzed 6.29 million Chicago bike-share trips to learn how casual riders differ from annual members. Members take 64.4% of rides, but casuals ride far longer at 20.1 minutes on average against 12.3 for members. Weekends belong to casuals, summer is casual season, and Navy Pier alone starts 50,894 casual rides.

My takeaway: the membership pitch belongs on the lakefront corridor on summer weekends, aimed at long-ride casuals whose habits already justify joining.

I cleaned the data with Python and DuckDB, ran the analysis in SQL, and charted it in Python and Tableau.
